Frequently Asked Questions

What are Roman Numerals? ▼

Roman numerals are a numeral system originating in ancient Rome that uses combinations of letters from the Latin alphabet (I, V, X, L, C, D, M) to represent numbers. Each letter has a specific value: I=1, V=5, X=10, L=50, C=100, D=500, M=1000.

How do I convert a number to Roman numerals? ▼

Use our free converter tools! Simply enter your number, date, or name, and we'll instantly convert it to Roman numerals. Our advanced algorithm handles all values from 1 to 3999 accurately.

What's the largest number in Roman numerals? ▼

In standard Roman numeral notation, the largest commonly used number is 3,999 (MMMCMXCIX). However, ancient Romans used a vinculum (overline) to multiply numbers by 1,000, allowing for larger values.

What makes Roman numerals special? ▼

Roman numerals are iconic symbols of ancient civilization. They're used in formal documents, clock faces, movie credits, and more. Converting your birth date or name to Roman numerals creates a timeless, elegant representation that's perfect for sharing!
