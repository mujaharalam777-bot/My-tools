What is Public Provident Fund and Why is it Important?

PPF or Public Provident Fund is a government-backed savings scheme with 15 year lock-in. It comes with EEE tax benefit - Exempt on Investment, Exempt on Interest, Exempt on Maturity. For FY 2026-27 the rate is 7.1%. The maximum investment allowed is ₹1,50,000 per financial year and minimum is ₹500.

The biggest advantage of PPF is safety + tax free returns. Unlike FD, PPF interest is not taxable. Unlike ELSS or Mutual Fund, there is zero market risk. This is why every Indian should use a ppf calculator 2026 before investing.

How Does PPF Calculator Work? Formula Explained

PPF interest is calculated monthly but credited yearly. Interest for a month is calculated on the lowest balance between 5th and last day of that month. This is why the ppf 1st april investment hack works.

Interest = Principal × Rate × Time

Example: If you deposit ₹1,50,000 on 1st April every year for 15 years at 7.1%, your maturity will be approximately ₹40,68,000. Total invested = ₹22,50,000. Interest earned = ₹18,18,000. Use our ppf maturity calculator above to get exact numbers for any amount and tenure.

1st April Hack: How to Earn ₹11,340 Extra Interest

This is the most important PPF strategy that 90% people miss. Because interest is calculated from 5th of every month, depositing your full ₹1.5L before 5th April gives you 12 months interest in that year. Depositing in December gives you only 4 months interest.

Over 15 years, this small timing difference creates ₹11,340 to ₹15,000 extra depending on rate. Always use our "1st April Hack" tab to see the exact difference for your amount.

PPF Missed Installment Penalty & Revival Calculator

What if you miss depositing for 1 or 2 years? Your PPF account becomes inactive. To revive it you need to pay:

  • Minimum ₹500 for each missed year
  • Penalty of ₹50 for each missed year

Example: If you missed 2 years, you need to pay 2 × 500 + 2 × 50 = ₹1,100 to revive. Use our "Missed / Revival" tab to calculate exact amount. After revival, you can continue and get full maturity benefits.

PPF Loan Calculator - 3rd to 6th Year Double Dip Strategy

From 3rd financial year to 6th financial year, you can take loan up to 25% of balance of 2nd year preceding. PPF loan interest is only 1% above PPF rate. So at 7.1% PPF, loan rate is 8.1%.

Smart strategy: Take loan for emergency and if you have extra income, repay it fast. Or if you have unused annual limit, you can redeposit the loan amount back into PPF. Our ppf loan calculator tab shows if this "double dip" is profitable for you.

30 Year PPF Extension Plan: 15 + 5 + 5 + 5

After 15 years, you can extend PPF in blocks of 5 years. You have 2 options:

  1. With Contribution: Continue depositing up to ₹1.5L every year
  2. Without Contribution: Just let existing balance earn interest

If you invest ₹1.5L for 15 years and then extend 15 more years with contribution, maturity can cross ₹1.5 Crore at 7.1%. Use our "30Y Extension" tab to see year-by-year chart.
