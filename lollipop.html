What is Bionic Reading?

Bionic Reading is a typography technique that bolds the first few letters of each word (the fixation point). Your brain uses those anchors to “fill in” the rest of the word, allowing faster reading with less visual effort. This free bionic reading converter turns any text into bionic format instantly — no signup, no limits.
