What is the Formula to Calculate Age?

Age = Current Date - Birth Date. We subtract years, then months, then days. If current day < birth day, we borrow 1 month. If current month < birth month, we borrow 1 year. This ensures exact years and months. You can also use this as age difference calculator and age between two dates tool.
