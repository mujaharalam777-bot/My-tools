How to Calculate FD Interest in India - Formula Explained

Banks in India mostly use compound interest. The formula is:

Maturity = Principal × (1 + Rate/100/Compounding)^ (Compounding × Years)

Example: ₹5,00,000 at 7% for 3 years, quarterly compounding = ₹5,00,000 × (1 + 0.07/4)^(4×3) = ₹6,15,475. Our fd interest calculator does this in 0.2 seconds.

If you choose monthly payout fd, interest is paid every month but compounding effect is slightly less. For maximum growth, choose cumulative FD with quarterly compounding.

FD Premature Withdrawal Penalty - How Much Will You Lose?

Need money urgently? Breaking FD hurts. Banks charge 0.5% to 1% penalty on the applicable rate for the period held.

Example: You booked ₹5L FD at 7.25% for 5 years. After 2 years you break it. Bank gives you interest at 6.25% instead of 7.25%. Your loss = ₹14,000+ plus penalty. Use our fd premature calculator tab to know exact loss before breaking.

Tip: Instead of breaking, take loan against fd at 9%. Your FD continues to earn 7% and you pay only 2% net.

TDS on FD Interest - Section 194A Explained Simply

This is where most people get shocked. As per Income Tax Act 194A:

  • If total FD interest in one bank in one financial year exceeds ₹40,000, bank deducts 10% TDS.
  • For senior citizen fd, limit is ₹50,000.
  • TDS is PAN based. If you don't give PAN, TDS @20%.

Solution: If your total income is below taxable limit, submit Form 15G if age <60 or Form 15H if age 60+. Our tool calculates expected interest and warns you in TDS tab. You can also download the form directly.

FD Ladder Strategy - Best Way to Get Yearly Liquidity

Instead of putting ₹10L in one 5 year FD, split it: ₹2L in 1yr, ₹2L in 2yr, ₹2L in 3yr, ₹2L in 4yr, ₹2L in 5yr. This is called FD Ladder.

Benefits: Every year one FD matures. You get liquidity + you lock part of money for longer tenure at higher rate. Use our fd ladder calculator to build custom ladder in 1 click.
