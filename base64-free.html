❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    
Is this Base64 Encoder free?
Yes. Completely free with no limits, no registration and no watermarks. Everything runs in your browser.

    
Does my data get uploaded to a server?
No. The tool is 100% client-side. Your text and files never leave your device.

    
Can I encode images and PDFs?
Yes. Drag & drop any image, PDF, ZIP or video. You can also compress images before encoding.

    
What is URL-safe Base64?
URL-safe Base64 replaces + and / with - and _ so the string can be used safely in URLs and filenames.

    
How does the JWT Decoder work?
Paste any JWT. The tool splits Header, Payload and Signature, then base64url-decodes and pretty-prints the JSON.

    
Is there a file size limit?
No hard limit. Very large files are limited only by your browser memory. Image compression helps with big photos.

    
Does it work offline?
Yes. After the first load the page works without internet. It is PWA-ready.

    
How to use keyboard shortcuts?
Ctrl+Enter = Encode, Ctrl+Shift+Enter = Decode, Ctrl+K = Clear all fields.
