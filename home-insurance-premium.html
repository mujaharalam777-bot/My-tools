❓ Frequently Asked Questions (20)

1. How is home insurance premium calculated in India?

Premium depends on carpet area, city, building age, construction type, structure sum insured, content sum insured and selected add-ons (earthquake, flood, STFI, theft).

2. Does basic home insurance cover flood and earthquake?

Usually no. Standard fire policy excludes flood, inundation and earthquake. These must be purchased as add-ons.

3. What is under-insurance penalty?

If sum insured is lower than actual value, the insurer applies average clause and pays only a proportionate part of the claim.

4. Is home insurance compulsory with home loan?

Most banks mandate structure (fire) cover for the loan tenure. Content and catastrophe covers are optional but recommended.

5. Can tenants buy home insurance?

Yes. Renters insurance covers contents — furniture, electronics, clothes, appliances. Premiums can start from ₹299–₹999 per year.

6. How long does a home insurance claim take?

Many straightforward claims are settled in 7–21 working days after complete documentation and survey.

7. Building cover vs Content cover — difference?

Building covers the structure. Content covers movable items inside the house.

8. Is home insurance premium eligible for 80C?

Generally no. Home loan principal (80C) and interest (Section 24) give tax benefits. Insurance protects the asset itself.

9. Which companies are popular for home insurance in 2026?

Tata AIG, HDFC Ergo, ICICI Lombard, Bajaj Allianz, New India, SBI General, Go Digit and others.

10. How to avoid claim rejection?

Adequate sum insured, correct add-ons, quick intimation, proper documents and understanding exclusions.

11. What is STFI cover?

Storm, Tempest, Flood and Inundation — important for many Indian cities during monsoon.

12. Do I need separate jewellery cover?

High-value jewellery often has sub-limits under content. Consider a separate all-risk cover for expensive items.

13. Does home insurance cover terrorism?

Some policies offer terrorism as an optional cover. Check the specific product wording.

14. Can I transfer home insurance when selling the house?

Yes, policies can usually be transferred to the new owner with insurer consent.

15. What is reinstatement value vs market value?

Reinstatement value is the cost to rebuild as new. It is preferred for adequate protection.

16. Is there a waiting period in home insurance?

Usually no long waiting period like health insurance, but some conditions and exclusions apply from day one.

17. Can I buy home insurance online?

Yes. Most major insurers and aggregators allow online purchase and policy download.

18. Does home insurance cover neighbouring flat damage?

Liability to third parties may need a separate liability section or add-on. Check policy.

19. How often should I revise sum insured?

Ideally every year or after major renovation / purchase of expensive items.
