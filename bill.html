What is a PWA (Progressive Web App)?

A Progressive Web App is a website that utilizes modern web capabilities to deliver an app-like experience to users. These apps deploy safely to any device—iOS, Android, Windows, or Mac—directly from the browser. They bypass the traditional app store monopolies, eliminating the 30% revenue cuts and the tedious weeks-long review processes. A PWA looks, feels, and operates exactly like a native app. It sits on the user's home screen, works offline, and supports advanced features like push notifications.

Why Convert Your Website to an App?

The transition from a standard website to a mobile application carries immense benefits. First, it radically increases user retention. When your icon sits on a user's home screen, they are 300% more likely to return to your platform. Second, performance is unparalleled. By utilizing a Service Worker (which our tool automatically generates for you), your app caches essential assets. This means it loads instantaneously, even on terrible network connections or complete offline states.

Furthermore, building a native iOS and Android app traditionally costs upwards of $50,000 and requires maintaining two separate codebases. By converting your existing responsive website into a PWA, you leverage your current web infrastructure. Updates are instant. Whenever you update your website, the app automatically reflects those changes without the user needing to download an update from an app store.
