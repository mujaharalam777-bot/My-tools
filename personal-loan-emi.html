Complete Guide to Personal Loan 2026

By Mujahar, Certified Financial Analyst

A personal loan is an unsecured loan that can be used for any legitimate purpose – medical emergency, wedding, travel, debt consolidation or home renovation. Because there is no collateral, interest rates are higher than home or car loans, but approval is faster and documentation lighter.

How Personal Loan EMI is Calculated

Banks use the standard reducing-balance formula: EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N – 1]. P is the loan amount, R is the monthly interest rate and N is the number of monthly instalments. This calculator applies the exact formula so your numbers match bank schedules.

Interest Rates in 2026

Public and private sector banks typically offer personal loans from around 10.5%–12.5% for applicants with strong CIBIL scores. Instant digital lenders and NBFCs may start from 13%–18% but disburse within hours. Always compare the annual percentage rate and processing fee, not just the headline rate.

Eligibility & CIBIL

Most lenders look at monthly income, existing obligations (FOIR), employment stability and credit score. A CIBIL score of 750+ significantly improves approval odds and pricing. Scores above 800 often unlock the lowest slabs. Use the eligibility and CIBIL tools on this page for a quick estimate.

Debt Consolidation

If you are paying high interest on multiple credit cards or small loans, consolidating them into one personal loan at a lower rate can reduce total interest and simplify payments. Run the numbers carefully including any foreclosure charges on existing facilities.

Prepayment & Foreclosure

Many lenders allow part-prepayment or full foreclosure after a lock-in period. A small penalty may apply, but the interest saved is frequently much larger. Check your agreement and use the pre-closure calculator before deciding.

Practical Tips

Borrow only what you need, keep tenure as short as cash-flow allows, improve CIBIL before applying, and read the fine print on processing fees, insurance add-ons and late-payment charges. This Personal Loan Calculator Pro is designed to give you clear numbers so you can decide with confidence.

FAQ – Personal Loan EMI Calculator

What is the interest rate for personal loan in SBI 2026?

Indicative rates start around 10.75% for well-qualified applicants. Final rate depends on CIBIL, income and relationship with the bank.

How much personal loan can I get on 50,000 salary?

Roughly 10–20 times net monthly salary depending on FOIR, city and credit score. Use the Eligibility AI tab for a personalised estimate.

Is instant personal loan more expensive?

Often yes. Faster disbursal from NBFCs usually comes with higher rates. Compare total cost before choosing speed over price.

Can self-employed get personal loan?

Yes. Lenders assess ITR, business vintage and bank credits. Rates and documents differ from salaried applicants.

Does prepaying personal loan save money?

In most cases yes, especially in the early years when interest component is high. Factor in any foreclosure charges.
