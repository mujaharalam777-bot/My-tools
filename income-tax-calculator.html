Budget 2026 Income Tax Guide – Complete Analysis for FY 2026-27

The Union Budget 2026 retained the progressive structure introduced earlier while bringing the new Income-tax Act, 2025 into force from 1 April 2026. For Assessment Year 2027-28 corresponding to Financial Year 2026-27, the new tax regime remains the default option for individuals, HUFs and other specified persons. Taxpayers may still opt for the old regime if their deductions under Chapter VI-A and exemptions make it more beneficial.

Income Tax Slab 2026-27 – New Tax Regime

The slab rates under section 115BAC for FY 2026-27 are:

  • Up to ₹4,00,000 – Nil
  • ₹4,00,001 to ₹8,00,000 – 5%
  • ₹8,00,001 to ₹12,00,000 – 10%
  • ₹12,00,001 to ₹16,00,000 – 15%
  • ₹16,00,001 to ₹20,00,000 – 20%
  • ₹20,00,001 to ₹24,00,000 – 25%
  • Above ₹24,00,000 – 30%

A standard deduction of ₹75,000 is available to salaried individuals and pensioners. Rebate under section 87A continues at up to ₹60,000 for resident individuals whose total income does not exceed ₹12 lakh, effectively making income up to approximately ₹12.75 lakh tax-free after standard deduction.

Old Tax Regime Calculator Perspective

The old regime slabs remain unchanged: Nil up to ₹2.5 lakh (₹3 lakh for senior citizens, ₹5 lakh for super senior citizens), 5% up to ₹5 lakh, 20% up to ₹10 lakh and 30% thereafter. Standard deduction is ₹50,000. Full suite of deductions under sections 80C (₹1.5 lakh), 80D, 80CCD(1B), 24(b) home-loan interest up to ₹2 lakh on self-occupied property, HRA exemption under section 10(13A), and many others continue to be available. Rebate under 87A is limited to ₹12,500 when total income does not exceed ₹5 lakh.

HRA Exemption Calculator Logic

House Rent Allowance exemption is the least of: (a) actual HRA received, (b) rent paid minus 10% of salary (basic + DA), (c) 50% of salary in metro cities or 40% in non-metro cities. This exemption is available only under the old tax regime. Employees living in their own house or paying no rent cannot claim HRA exemption.

Capital Gain Tax Calculator – Shares, Property & Crypto

Long-term capital gains on listed equity and equity-oriented mutual funds exceeding ₹1.25 lakh are taxed at 12.5% without indexation. Short-term capital gains on equity attract 20%. Property long-term capital gains are taxed at 20% with indexation benefit in most cases. Virtual Digital Assets including cryptocurrency are taxed at a flat 30% under section 115BBH; only cost of acquisition is deductible and loss cannot be set off against any other income.

Business Income & 44AD / 44ADA

Presumptive taxation under section 44AD allows eligible businesses with turnover up to ₹3 crore (subject to conditions) to declare 6% or 8% of turnover as income. Professionals under 44ADA can declare 50% of gross receipts. These incomes are added to total income and taxed at normal slab rates. F&O trading is generally treated as business income and can be set off under normal provisions.

NRI Tax Calculator Considerations

Non-resident Indians are taxed only on Indian-sourced income. TDS under section 195 applies on many payments to NRIs. Capital gains on Indian assets, rental income and interest from NRO accounts are taxable. NRIs cannot claim the 87A rebate. Special rates and treaty benefits under DTAA may reduce the final liability.

Tax Saving Calculator 2026 – Practical Strategy

For most salaried taxpayers with limited investments, the new regime is simpler and often cheaper because of the higher basic exemption and standard deduction. However, if you already have or can comfortably invest ₹1.5 lakh under 80C, pay home-loan interest, claim HRA and medical insurance, the old regime may still win. Always run both calculations with actual numbers before the end of the financial year.

ITR Filing & Form 16

Form 16 issued by the employer summarises salary, TDS and deductions reported. You can use OCR tools to extract figures, but always cross-verify with Form 26AS and Annual Information Statement (AIS). ITR-1 (Sahaj) is for simple salary + one house property cases; ITR-2 is required when there are capital gains or more than one house property; ITR-4 is for presumptive business income.

Interest & Penalty under 234A, 234B, 234C

Late filing of return attracts fee under section 234F (₹1,000 or ₹5,000 depending on income and delay). Interest under 234A applies on tax unpaid after due date. 234B is charged for shortfall in advance tax beyond 90% and 234C for deferment of instalments. Paying advance tax correctly and filing on time remains the cheapest compliance strategy.

What is New in Income Tax for FY 2026-27?

The Union Budget 2026 made the new tax regime the default option. The new Income Tax Act 2025 came into effect from 1st April 2026. Here are the key changes for Assessment Year 2027-28:

  • Standard Deduction: Increased to ₹75,000 under new regime for salaried and pensioners.
  • 87A Rebate: Tax is zero up to ₹12,75,000 for salaried people under new regime after standard deduction.

HRA Exemption Calculator Logic Explained

If you live in rented house, you can claim HRA exemption under old regime only. Exemption is least of 3 things:

1. Actual HRA received from employer

2. Rent Paid - 10% of Basic + DA

3. 50% of Basic + DA for Metro, 40% for Non-Metro

Example: If Basic is ₹6L, HRA ₹2.4L, Rent ₹3L in Metro. Exemption = Min(2.4L, 3L-60k, 3L) = ₹2.4L

Tax on Capital Gains and Crypto FY 2026-27

Investment income is taxed differently:

  • STCG Equity: 20% flat
  • LTCG Equity: 12.5% above ₹1.25L gain per year
  • Property LTCG: 20% with indexation
  • Crypto/VDA: 30% flat + 1% TDS. No loss set-off allowed.

Section 80C, 80D, NPS Deduction List Old Regime

To save tax under old regime, use these deductions:

  • 80C: PPF, ELSS, LIC, EPF, Home Loan Principal. Limit ₹1.5 Lakh
  • 80D: Health Insurance. ₹25K for self, ₹50K for senior parents
  • 80CCD(1B): Extra NPS deduction ₹50,000
  • 24b: Home Loan Interest up to ₹2 Lakh
