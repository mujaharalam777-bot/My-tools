❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1. How much term insurance cover do I need?

A practical starting point is 15–20 times annual income plus outstanding loans. Use the “How Much Cover” tab for a quick estimate.

2. What is the premium for 1 crore term insurance?

For a healthy 30-year-old non-smoking male, monthly premium for 1 Cr / 30 years often falls in the ₹800–₹1,400 range depending on the company.

3. Is term insurance better than endowment?

For pure protection, yes. Term gives high cover at low cost. Endowment mixes insurance with low-yielding savings.

4. Should I buy Return of Premium (ROP)?

ROP costs much more. The extra amount invested in equity/index funds usually creates higher wealth than the returned premium.

5. Does term insurance give 80C benefit?

Yes. Premium is eligible under Section 80C within the overall ₹1.5 lakh limit.

6. Do females get lower premium?

Yes. Women typically get 10–15% lower rates due to higher life expectancy.

7. How much extra do smokers pay?

Smokers can pay 40–80% more (sometimes higher) depending on age and insurer underwriting.

8. Which companies have high claim settlement?

Check latest IRDAI data. Many private insurers and LIC have published ratios above 95–98% in recent years.

9. Is medical test compulsory?

For higher covers or older ages, yes. Younger ages with lower covers may get simplified underwriting.

10. Can I increase cover later?

Some plans offer life-stage or top-up options. Otherwise you may need a new policy.

11. What is Critical Illness rider?

It pays a lump sum on diagnosis of specified critical illnesses. Useful if you have limited health cover.

12. What is Accidental Death Benefit?

Extra payout if death occurs due to accident. Relatively low cost rider.

13. Can NRIs buy term insurance from India?

Yes, many insurers allow NRI purchase subject to rules and medicals.

14. Is there a waiting period?

Suicide exclusion is typically 12 months. Other conditions depend on the product.

15. Can I port my term policy?

Switching is possible in some cases; underwriting and outstanding term matter.

16. Online vs offline buying?

Online is usually cheaper and faster. Offline may help if you need guidance.

17. Does occupation affect premium?

Yes. High-risk jobs (industrial, driver, defence in some cases) can attract extra loading.

18. What happens if I stop paying premium?

Pure term plans usually lapse without value. ROP/endowment may have surrender value after a few years.

19. Is this calculator free and private?

Yes. 100% free, no login. All calculations run in your browser.

20. Best age to buy term insurance?

As early as possible after you have dependents or liabilities. Premiums rise with age.
