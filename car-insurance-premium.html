❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1. How is car insurance premium calculated?

IDV, age of vehicle, RTO/city, fuel type, NCB, voluntary deductible and add-ons mainly decide the premium.

2. What is IDV?

Insured Declared Value is the maximum own-damage claim amount, based on ex-showroom price minus depreciation.

3. What is Zero Depreciation cover?

It pays claim on parts without depreciation deduction. Highly useful for new cars.

4. How does NCB work?

Claim-free years give 20% → 25% → 35% → 45% → 50% discount on own-damage premium.

5. Is third-party insurance enough?

It is mandatory by law but does not cover damage to your own car. Comprehensive is recommended.

6. Can I transfer NCB to new car?

Yes, subject to insurer rules when you sell the old car and buy a new one within the allowed time.

7. What is engine protection add-on?

Covers engine damage due to water ingress (flood) etc., which is otherwise excluded.

8. Does electric car insurance cost more?

Battery and components can make OD premium different; many insurers now have specific EV rates.

9. When should I renew car insurance?

Before expiry. Prefer starting the process 15–30 days early to avoid break in cover and NCB loss.

10. Are there hidden charges?

GST is always applicable. Some platforms may show convenience or inspection fees — always check final breakup.

11. Bike insurance on same tool?

Yes. Switch to Bike mode for two-wheeler indicative premium.

12. Which company is best?

Compare claim ratio, cashless garages, price and add-on options. No single best for everyone.

13. Can I buy online?

Yes. Most insurers and aggregators allow full online purchase and policy download.

14. What is voluntary deductible?

You agree to bear a fixed amount in each claim in exchange for lower premium.

15. Is this calculator free and private?

Yes. 100% free, no login. All calculations run in your browser.

3. What is IDV and How to Calculate It?

IDV means Insured Declared Value. It is the maximum amount insurer will pay if your car is stolen or total loss. IRDAI gives depreciation chart: 0-6 months 5%, 1-2 years 20%, 3-4 years 40%, 5+ years 50%.

Example: If Ex-Showroom Price is ₹8,00,000 and car is 2 years old, IDV = ₹8,00,000 - 30% = ₹5,60,000. Use our IDV calculator above. Higher IDV = higher premium but better claim. For cheap car insurance for new car, don’t set IDV too low.

4. NCB Discount Explained: Save up to 50%

NCB = No Claim Bonus. If you don’t make any claim in a year, you get discount on Own Damage premium next year. Slab: 20% - 25% - 35% - 45% - 50%.

Tip: Even for small scratch, don’t claim if repair < ₹10,000. You will lose 5 years of NCB discount worth ₹25,000. You can also transfer NCB to new car. Check NCB saving in our calculator.

5. Zero Depreciation Cover: Is It Worth It?

If you searched for zero depreciation car insurance calculator mumbai, this is for you. Normally insurer deducts 30-50% depreciation on plastic, rubber, fiber parts. With Zero Depreciation cover, you get 100% claim.

Cost: Extra ₹3,000 - ₹5,000 per year. Benefit: In one bumper claim of ₹45,000, you save ₹15,000. Must buy for cars <5 years old. After 5 years, most companies stop offering it. Check Zero Dep Saver tab above.
