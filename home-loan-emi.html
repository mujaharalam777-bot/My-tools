How to use Home Loan Calculator in 2026

A home loan is usually the largest financial commitment most families make. The monthly EMI, total interest over 15–30 years, and tax benefits under sections 80C and 24(b) decide how affordable the house really is. This free Home Loan EMI Calculator 2026 is built to give you accurate numbers in seconds and help you compare banks before you apply.

Step 1 – Enter Loan Amount, Rate & Tenure

Start with the property value or the exact loan amount you need. Move the interest rate slider or pick a bank (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis). Set the tenure between 5 and 30 years. The calculator instantly shows monthly EMI, total interest and total amount payable using the standard reducing-balance formula.

Step 2 – Check Stamp Duty & Tax Benefit

Select your state to estimate stamp duty (West Bengal 7%, Delhi 6%, Maharashtra 5%, etc.). The tool also shows approximate annual tax benefit under old regime – up to ₹1.5 lakh principal under 80C and up to ₹2 lakh interest under section 24(b) for self-occupied property.

Step 3 – Run Prepayment Scenarios

Paying an extra ₹1 lakh in year 2 can easily save ₹3–5 lakh in interest over a long tenure. Enter the year and amount in the prepayment section to see exact interest saved and how many months the loan shortens.

Step 4 – Compare Banks & Download PDF

The bank comparison table shows EMI and total interest for the same loan across SBI, HDFC and ICICI at current 2026 indicative rates. Click “Apply Rate” to lock that rate into the calculator. Finally download the full amortization schedule as a PDF named with your name for records or bank discussions.

Eligibility Tip for ₹60,000 Salary

Most lenders keep FOIR (fixed obligation to income ratio) around 40–50%. On a net salary of ₹60,000 with no other EMIs, a rough maximum home loan eligibility is ₹35–50 lakh depending on rate, tenure and credit score. Always confirm with the bank’s official eligibility calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is home loan EMI calculated in 2026?

EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] ÷ [(1+R)^N – 1]. P = principal, R = monthly rate, N = months. This tool uses the exact formula.

What is the SBI / HDFC home loan rate today?

Indicative starting rates in mid-2026: SBI ~7.25%, HDFC ~7.75%, ICICI ~7.90%. Actual offer depends on profile.

How much loan on 60,000 salary?

Roughly ₹35–50 lakh at 40–50% FOIR for 20–25 year tenure, subject to credit score and other obligations.

What tax benefits do I get under 80C and 24B?

80C: up to ₹1.5 lakh on principal. 24(b): up to ₹2 lakh on interest for self-occupied house (old regime).

Can I download the amortization schedule as PDF?

Yes. Click “Download Amortization PDF”. The file is named Home-Loan-Schedule-[YourName].pdf.
