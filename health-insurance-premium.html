❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1. How is health insurance premium calculated?

Age, sum insured, city zone, number of members, PED and add-ons mainly decide the premium.

2. What is room rent capping?

Many plans limit room rent to 1% of SI per day. Higher room can reduce the entire claim proportionately.

3. What is co-pay?

You pay a fixed % of the approved claim. Common in senior plans or specific city variants.

4. Does health insurance give 80D benefit?

Yes. Premium for self, family and parents is eligible under Section 80D with higher limits for seniors.

5. Can I buy policy with pre-existing disease?

Yes. PED is usually covered after waiting period (2–4 years). Some plans offer shorter waits at higher cost.

6. What is family floater?

One sum insured shared by all family members. Usually cheaper than separate individual covers.

7. Cashless vs Reimbursement?

Cashless = hospital settles with insurer on network. Reimbursement = you pay first, claim later.

8. Is maternity covered?

Only if the plan has maternity benefit and after the specified waiting period.

9. How long is initial waiting period?

Usually 30 days for illness (accidents often covered from day 1).

10. Can I port my health policy?

Yes. Portability allows switching insurer while carrying waiting period credit.

11. Best sum insured for family of 3?

₹10–25 lakh is common starting point in metros; higher if you prefer private rooms / big cities.

12. Does COVID need separate cover?

Most comprehensive plans now cover COVID like other illnesses. Check exclusions.

13. What is restoration benefit?

Sum insured can refill once in a year after a claim (conditions apply).

14. Are daycare procedures covered?

Modern plans cover many daycare treatments that do not need 24-hour hospitalisation.

15. Is this calculator free and private?

Yes. 100% free, no login. All calculations run in your browser only.
